## Assembler

- The x86-64 assembler of inline assembly, file-scope asm and assembly sources
  (`cmd/axiom/src/asm/`, no other tool needed) encodes the general-purpose and system
  instruction sets (`cli`, `wrmsr`, `mov` to and from control and debug registers, ...),
  the x87 FPU, MMX, SSE through SSE4.2 and their AVX (VEX) forms, AVX2, FMA, F16C, BMI,
  the whole of AVX-512 (F, VL, BW, DQ, CD, IFMA, VBMI, VBMI2, BITALG, VNNI, VPOPCNTDQ,
  VP2INTERSECT, BF16, FP16, ER, PF, 4FMAPS, 4VNNIW) with masking (`{%k1}{z}`), broadcast
  (`{1to16}`), embedded rounding (`{rn-sae}`) and `{sae}`, the VEX instructions of
  AVX-VNNI, AVX-VNNI-INT8, AVX-VNNI-INT16, AVX-IFMA, AVX-NE-CONVERT, SHA512, SM3, SM4,
  CMPccXADD and FMA4, and AMX (`tmm0`-`tmm7`, the tile configuration, loads, stores and
  dot products of AMX-BF16, AMX-INT8, AMX-FP16 and AMX-COMPLEX), in both syntaxes. The
  tables are generated by `cmd/internal/tools/gen_x64_table.py` (with
  `gen_x64_table_vec.py` and `gen_x64_table_more.py`). Not yet encoded (C3097): APX, XOP
  and some legacy and system instructions (docs/c.md, "Known gaps").
- The Intel syntax: `.intel_syntax noprefix` switches to it (`.att_syntax` back), and
  `--c-asm-dialect=intel` makes it the syntax of every template, file-scope asm and
  assembly source, selects the second alternative of `{att|intel}` in templates and
  writes operands as that syntax has them (`DWORD PTR [rbp-8]`, `OFFSET FLAT:sym`). The
  comparison pseudo-ops (`vcmpltps`, `vpcmpnleud` ...) are accepted in both syntaxes. The
  rounding operand follows the conventional placement (`vcvtusi2ss %eax, {rn-sae}, %xmm0,
  %xmm1`), and the Intel syntax's `WORD BCST` / `DWORD BCST` / `QWORD BCST` must name the
  instruction's element size.
- The constraint `A` puts an `__int128` operand in the rdx:rax pair; `tmm0`-`tmm7` are
  clobbers.
- Fixed: `mov $imm, %ah` (and `%bh`, `%ch`, `%dh`) was encoded with the wrong register.
